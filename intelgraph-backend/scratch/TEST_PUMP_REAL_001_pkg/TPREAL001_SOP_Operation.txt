BHARAT REFINERY PVT LTD
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

SOP Reference: BRPL-SOP-UNT450-002
Title: Startup, Normal Operation & Shutdown — Hydrocarbon Feed Pump TEST-PUMP-REAL-001
Revision: Rev 3
Applicable Equipment: TEST-PUMP-REAL-001

1. PURPOSE
----------
This procedure defines the startup, normal operation, and emergency shutdown steps for pump TEST-PUMP-REAL-001
in Unit 450 CDU crude preheat train service.

2. OPERATING LIMITS
--------------------
Minimum Operating Flow: 90 m3/h (minimum continuous flow — do not operate below without bypass)
Maximum Operating Pressure: 29.47 bar
Maximum Bearing Temperature: 85 deg C (DE), 82 deg C (NDE)
Maximum Vibration: 5.0 mm/s RMS (trip setpoint)
Normal Discharge Pressure Range: 25–28 bar
Normal Suction Pressure Range: 1.8–2.6 bar
Seal Pot Minimum Level: 65% (low alarm 60%, trip at 55%)

3. CRITICAL SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
--------------------------------
- Do not start pump with discharge valve fully closed.
- Do not operate below minimum continuous flow (MCF) of 90 m3/h without minimum flow bypass BRPL-MFV-450A open.
- If bearing temperature exceeds 80 deg C, increase monitoring frequency. Trip at 85 deg C.
- If vibration exceeds 3.5 mm/s, escalate to Reliability Engineer immediately.

4. STARTUP PROCEDURE
---------------------
Step 1: Check suction strainer dP — must be < 0.20 bar before startup. If > 0.20 bar, clean strainer.
Step 2: Confirm seal pot level > 65%.
Step 3: Open suction valve (HV-450A) fully. Crack discharge valve (HV-450B) open 10%.
Step 4: Confirm minimum flow bypass (BRPL-MFV-450A) is open.
Step 5: Start motor from MCC panel or DCS.
Step 6: Gradually open discharge valve to achieve required flow 312 m3/h.
Step 7: Close minimum flow bypass once flow confirmed > 100 m3/h.
Step 8: Log startup time, initial pressure and temperature readings in the shift log.

5. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN
----------------------
On any of:
- DE/NDE bearing temperature > 85 / 82 deg C
- Vibration > 5.0 mm/s
- Seal pot level < 55%
- Process LOW-LOW flow interlock (FSLL-450A) activation

Immediate Actions:
1. Initiate standby pump start (automatic or manual).
2. Confirm standby pump has taken over duty.
3. Isolate TEST-PUMP-REAL-001 from suction and discharge.
4. Notify Maintenance and Reliability.
5. Complete Shift Log entry with timestamp and reason.
