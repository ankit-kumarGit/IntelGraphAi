INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
--------------------
At 14:23 hrs on 17-Jan-2026, pump TEST-PUMP-REAL-001 tripped on LOW FLOW interlock (FSLL-450A, setpoint 85 m3/h).
Operating flow had been declining slowly since 11:00 hrs (from 312 m3/h to 89 m3/h over ~3 hours).
DCS historian confirmed gradual reduction in suction pressure from 2.4 bar to 1.1 bar.
Field operator dispatched; reported suction strainer Y-450A was heavily fouled with polymer deposits.
Strainer pressure differential exceeded 0.45 bar (high alarm set at 0.30 bar but alarm card was suppressed — see CA-2).
Pump itself was found intact; no mechanical damage.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS — 5-WHY
-----------------------------
Why 1: Why did the pump trip? Low flow interlock activated at 85 m3/h.
Why 2: Why was flow low? Suction pressure dropped below cavitation threshold due to fouled suction strainer Y-450A.
Why 3: Why was the strainer fouled? Polymer carry-over from upstream vessel V-450 during abnormal distillation conditions (cold feed event on 16-Jan-2026 night shift).
Why 4: Why was strainer fouling not caught earlier? dP transmitter alarm was suppressed in DCS following a nuisance alarm campaign on 14-Jan-2026. Suppression was not time-limited.
Why 5: Why was alarm suppression not time-limited? Plant alarm management procedure BRPL-PROC-AM-004 does not require a mandatory review interval for non-safety suppressed alarms.

ROOT CAUSE (ASSESSED)
---------------------
Root Cause: Indefinitely suppressed dP alarm on suction strainer Y-450A masked progressive fouling.

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
------------------
CA-1: Strainer Y-450A cleaned and returned to service. — Status: Complete (17-Jan-2026)
CA-2: dP alarm suppression on PDSH-450A restored and alarm investigated. — Status: Complete (17-Jan-2026)
CA-3: Procedure BRPL-PROC-AM-004 revised to mandate 48-hour max suppression window for non-safety alarms. — Owner: Instrument Dept. Due: 28-Feb-2026 — Status: Open
CA-4: Cold-feed event protocol to trigger mandatory strainer inspection within 2 hours of event. — Owner: Operations. Due: 15-Feb-2026 — Status: Open
