PROCESS DESCRIPTION
--------------------
TEST-PUMP-REAL-001 is the duty hydrocarbon feed pump in Unit 450 Crude Distillation Unit (CDU).
It transfers desalted crude oil from the desalter drum D-450 to the crude preheat train (exchangers E-450A through E-450F)
before entry into the atmospheric distillation column T-450.

CIRCUIT SUMMARY
---------------
Source: Desalter Drum D-450 — Operating Pressure 1.8–2.6 bar, Temperature 135–155 deg C
Pump: TEST-PUMP-REAL-001 — Centrifugal, rated 312 m3/h at 178 m head
Destination: Preheat Train Inlet — Target Pressure 27–28 bar at 312 m3/h

KEY ASSOCIATED INSTRUMENTS
---------------------------
FSLL-450A  — Low-Low Flow Interlock (trip at 85 m3/h)
PDSH-450A  — Suction Strainer High dP Alarm (alarm at 0.30 bar, suppressed on 14-Jan-2026 — see BRPL-INC-2026-0117)
TE-450-DE  — DE Bearing Temperature Transmitter (alarm 80 deg C, trip 85 deg C)
TE-450-NDE — NDE Bearing Temperature Transmitter (alarm 78 deg C, trip 82 deg C)
HV-450A    — Suction Block Valve
HV-450B    — Discharge Block Valve
BRPL-MFV-450A — Minimum Flow Bypass Valve (set at 90 m3/h recirculation)

STANDBY PUMP: TEST-PUMP-REAL-001B (identical duty standby in auto-start arrangement)
