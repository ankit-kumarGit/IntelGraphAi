CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL DESCRIPTION
---------------------------------
The BPWL-CFP-450-XHD is a single-stage, between-bearings centrifugal pump designed to API 610 (11th Edition), Type BB2. It is intended for continuous duty in hydrocarbon process services up to 270 deg C.

CHAPTER 2 — TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------
Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP): 33.0 bar g
Maximum Continuous Operating Pressure: 29.47 bar g
Minimum Continuous Flow (MCF): 90 m3/h — Do NOT operate below MCF without bypass recirculation open.
Maximum Suction Pressure: 6.0 bar g
Suction Strainer: Temporary strainer to be removed after 200 operating hours of commissioning flush.
Shaft Speed: 2975 rpm (nominal)
Bearing Lubrication: Shell Gadus S2 V220 grease, OEM-specified only. Do NOT mix greases.
DE Bearing Grease Quantity: 18 g per regreasing
NDE Bearing Grease Quantity: 14 g per regreasing
Regreasing Interval: Every 500 operating hours or 3 months, whichever is earlier.
Mechanical Seal: John Crane Type 2800 Tandem, Plan 53B
Seal Pot Barrier Fluid: ISO 46 mineral oil
Seal Pot Minimum Level: 65% (low level alarm at 60%)
Maximum Bearing Temperature (DE): 85 deg C (alarm at 80 deg C)
Maximum Bearing Temperature (NDE): 82 deg C (alarm at 78 deg C)
Maximum Vibration (Overall RMS): 3.5 mm/s alarm, 5.0 mm/s trip

CHAPTER 3 — STARTUP PROCEDURE (SUMMARY)
-----------------------------------------
1. Confirm suction valve fully open and discharge valve cracked.
2. Confirm seal pot level > 65%.
3. Start motor. Observe bearing temperatures and vibration for first 10 minutes.
4. Gradually open discharge valve to design flow.
5. Confirm minimum flow bypass is open if process flow < 90 m3/h.
